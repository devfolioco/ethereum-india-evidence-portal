# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Senior people at Indian banks, asset managers, exchanges, depositories, clearing corporations, NBFCs, fintechs and regulators, plus the researchers and builders who work with them. They are not crypto-native. They arrive from a link in an email, a colleague's forward or a search, usually at a desk, often between meetings, and decide in under a minute whether this is a serious source.

## Product Purpose
The site for ETHIndia Institutional, a think tank under ETHIndia that works out how Ethereum fits Indian institutional finance. Its jobs, in order:
1. Get people to the ETHIndia Institutional briefing/report and make it easy to read.
2. Drive invite requests for the Institutional Dinner (4 Nov 2026, Mumbai, invite-only).
3. Position ETHIndia Institutional as the neutral, credible front door to Ethereum for Indian institutions.

Success is a senior reader finishing a briefing module, or requesting a dinner invite, and coming away seeing ETHIndia Institutional as neutral rather than selling.

## Brand Personality
Calm, factual, trustworthy. A research institute that belongs to the ETHIndia family, not an event page. It speaks plainly, cites its numbers, and never hypes. Visually it borrows ethindia.co's language (white graph paper, black ink, pixel-font accents, ticker) and turns the volume down.

## Anti-references
- ethereuminstitutional.org's mistakes: simulated "live" data, invented hashes, decorative numbers, the org name as the H1, centred multi-paragraph copy, orbit/hub-and-spoke audience diagrams, timeline UI for non-sequential items, newsletter as the primary hero CTA, logo walls that outrank anchor supporters.
- Crypto-hype aesthetics: gradients, glow, neon accent blue, 3D coins.
- Indian motifs as centred ornament (mandala heroes). Motifs are cropped at an edge, one per section at most.
- Event-page energy: countdowns, urgency, loud colour.

## Design Principles
1. **Every number is real and sourced.** Nothing decorative, nothing simulated.
2. **Neutral, not selling.** Explain options; never pitch one.
3. **Reading first.** The briefing is the product. Long-form gets a quiet panel and a real measure.
4. **Quiet family resemblance.** Recognisably ETHIndia, with more whitespace and fewer motifs.
5. **Say it once.** No repeated claims across sections.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. No text under 13px, all text at least 4.5:1, no opacity-faded text. Interactive targets at least 44px, visible focus everywhere, keyboard-operable nav, ticker pause and FAQ. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops all animation. Real labels on every form input. Lighthouse 95+ on all four scores, mobile and desktop.
